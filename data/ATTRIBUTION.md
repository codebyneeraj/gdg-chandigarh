## Benchmark images (`data/bench/images/`)

41 photos from Wikimedia Commons, fetched with `scripts/fetch_bench_images.py`, which keeps only public-domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA files.
Images were downscaled to 800 px and re-encoded (metadata removed). Labels in `data/bench/labels.csv` are ours.
Photos that showed identifiable people or readable number plates were excluded from the set and not redistributed.
Share-alike images (CC BY-SA) remain under their original licence; the Apache-2.0 licence of this repo covers code only.

## Models

- Gemma 4 (`gemma-4-31b-it`, `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`) by Google DeepMind, open weights under Apache-2.0, called via the Gemini API.
- Gemini (`gemini-3.5-flash`) by Google, via the Gemini API (proprietary; used for ticket drafting).
