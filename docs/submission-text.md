# Registration form text

**Tagline (one line):**
Citizen waste photos in, prioritised and optimised collection routes out, with officials in control.

**Project description (150 words):**

SaafRoute turns citizen photos of overflowing bins and illegal dumping into prioritised, routed collection work for Chandigarh. A citizen uploads a photo and picks a sector. Gemma 4, Google's open-weight model, classifies the waste (type, material aligned with India's SWM Rules streams, severity) as strict, schema-validated JSON. Invalid answers are retried once and then sent to a human, never guessed. Gemini drafts a work ticket in English, Hindi or Punjabi. An official edits and approves it; the AI never dispatches. A transparent hotspot score ranks tickets by severity, age, repeat reports and nearby hospitals or markets. OR-Tools then plans capacity-aware truck routes and compares them with a naive fixed route. The fleet and demo data are synthetic and clearly labelled; savings are simulated. Photo metadata is stripped; photos showing faces or number plates are kept only blurred. Built on OpenStreetMap and open weights, any Indian city can self-host it. Apache-2.0.
